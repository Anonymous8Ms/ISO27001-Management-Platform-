
# Assurance and Review

## Purpose

This document defines how Ikigai collects evidence, performs internal reviews, conducts management review, and monitors GRC performance.

## Evidence Management

Evidence must:

- Be linked to a risk, control, SoA record, or treatment action.
- Identify the evidence owner.
- Include the date or period it covers.
- Be classified according to sensitivity.
- Have a review status.
- Be protected from unauthorized access or modification.
- Have an expiry or review date where applicable.
- Be retained according to business, legal, and audit requirements.

Evidence statuses:

- Draft
- Submitted
- Under Review
- Accepted
- Rejected
- Expired

## Internal Audit

Ikigai should periodically review whether:

- The ISMS follows its documented requirements.
- Controls are implemented as planned.
- Evidence is available and current.
- Risks and treatments are reviewed.
- SoA decisions remain accurate.
- Responsibilities are being performed.
- Previous findings have been corrected.

An auditor should remain sufficiently independent from the activity being reviewed. A person should not be the only auditor of their own work.

Internal-audit records should include:

- Audit scope
- Audit criteria
- Auditor
- Date
- Records reviewed
- Findings
- Corrective actions
- Responsible owners
- Target dates
- Follow-up results

## Management Review

Senior management should periodically review:

- Changes in internal and external issues.
- Risk status and treatment progress.
- Control performance.
- Security incidents.
- Audit findings.
- Corrective actions.
- Interested-party requirements.
- Resource and budget needs.
- Opportunities for improvement.

Management-review records should include:

- Meeting date
- Participants
- Information reviewed
- Decisions made
- Approved actions
- Action owners
- Target dates

## GRC Metrics

The platform should support metrics such as:

- Open risks by severity
- Overdue risk-treatment actions
- Controls by implementation status
- Evidence awaiting review
- Expired evidence
- Privileged-access reviews completed
- Vulnerabilities overdue for remediation
- Vendor reviews completed
- Security-training completion rate
- Incidents by severity
- Average incident-response time
- Open audit findings
- Corrective actions overdue

## Review Frequencies

- Critical risks: Monthly
- High risks: Monthly
- Normal risks: Quarterly
- Privileged-access review: Weekly
- Normal employee-access review: Monthly
- Critical and high-risk vendor review: Monthly
- Low-risk vendor review: Every three months
- Security awareness: Annual, with quarterly refreshers
- Internal audit: At least annually or according to the audit programme
- Management review: At least annually and after major changes or incidents
