
# Project Scope

## Organization Profile

**Organization name:** Ikigai 

**Industry:** Education Technology (EdTech)

**Organization description:**

Ikigai provides online courses, live classes, mock tests, and career-preparation programs for school and competitive-examination students. Students access the platform through web and mobile applications.

The company stores student names, contact details, age, educational information, learning progress, examination results, payment records, support requests, login credentials, and application activity logs. Some students may be minors. The company uses cloud infrastructure, payment service providers, video-conferencing tools, email services, and other technology vendors.

The main teams are Engineering, IT Operations, Academic Content, Customer Support, Sales and Marketing, Finance, Human Resources, Legal, and Management.

## Why the Organization Needs GRC

Ikigai needs Governance, Risk, and Compliance processes because it collects and processes sensitive student and customer information. The organization must define security responsibilities, manage information-security risks, protect student data, monitor vendors, and maintain evidence that security controls are operating properly.

The company also wants to satisfy customer expectations, contractual security requirements, privacy obligations, payment-security responsibilities, and future audit or certification requirements such as ISO/IEC 27001.

## Consequences of Poor Information-Security Management

If information security is poorly managed, unauthorized people could access student accounts, personal information, examination results, or payment-related data. A security incident could cause harm to students, loss of customer trust, complaints, financial loss, contractual problems, regulatory consequences, and damage to the company’s reputation.

## Main GRC Objectives

The organization wants to:

- Protect student and customer information.
- Manage information-security risks consistently.
- Define clear security responsibilities.
- Monitor third-party technology vendors.
- Maintain evidence for audits and customer reviews.
- Improve readiness for ISO/IEC 27001 certification.
- Support privacy and payment-security obligations.
