### 1. Student Information Databse 

**Asset owner: ** Ikigai IT operations / Data Protection Team 

** Information Stored : ** Student ID , contact info ,age ,parent info ,learning Progress etc

** Importance :** High 

** Security Concerns :** unauthorized access , accidental disclosure ,incorrect data modification and data loss

### 2. Student Accounts and Authentication system 

**Assest owner : ** Ikigai IT operations 

** Information Stored :** Username ,email address , password hasses ,login session etc 

**Importance : ** High

** security concerns : ** Account takeover ,unauthorized access , weak password etc

### 3. Course and Examination Content

**Asset owner:** Academic Content Team

**Information stored:** Course videos, study material, question banks, mock tests, answer keys, and examination content.

**Importance:** High

**Security concerns:** Unauthorized copying, leakage before an examination, unauthorized modification, and loss of intellectual property.

### 4. Employee Information

**Asset owner:** Human Resources

**Information stored:** Employee names, contact details, identification details, employment records, salary information, performance records, and access-related information.

**Importance:** High

**Security concerns:** Unauthorized access, insider disclosure, identity theft, improper sharing, and failure to remove access after an employee leaves.

### 5. Financial Information

**Asset owner:** Finance Department

**Information stored:** Revenue records, expenses, invoices, salary payments, refunds, tax information, budgets, and financial reports.

**Importance:** High

**Security concerns:** Unauthorized access, financial fraud, data manipulation, accidental disclosure, and loss of financial records.

### 6. Web and Mobile Learning Platform

**Asset owner:** Engineering and IT Operations

**Information processed:** Student accounts, course access, learning records, examination data, payment status, and application activity.

**Importance:** High

**Security concerns:** Service outage, unauthorized access, insecure features, data leakage, data alteration, and dependency on external services.
