# Risk Treatment Plan

## Purpose

This document defines how Ikigai will treat identified information-security risks, assign treatment actions, and review remaining residual risk.

## Risk Treatment Options

- **Treat:** Implement controls to reduce the likelihood or impact of a risk.
- **Avoid:** Stop the activity that creates the risk.
- **Transfer:** Share part of the risk through contracts, insurance, or third-party arrangements.
- **Accept:** Formally accept the remaining risk after reviewing its impact.

## Treatment Plan

### Risk 1: Unauthorized access to student information

**Treatment option:** Treat

**Treatment actions:**

- Apply least-privilege access.
- Use role-based permissions.
- Review database access regularly.
- Scan systems for vulnerabilities.
- Apply security patches promptly.
- Monitor and review security logs.
- Test application changes before release.

**Treatment owner:** Head of IT Operations

**Target status:** Planned

**Residual-risk review:** After access reviews, vulnerability scanning, and monitoring controls are implemented.

### Risk 2: Student account takeover

**Treatment option:** Treat

**Treatment actions:**

- Enforce strong password requirements.
- Implement multi-factor authentication where appropriate.
- Secure password-reset and account-recovery processes.
- Limit repeated failed login attempts.
- Monitor unusual login activity.
- Educate students and employees about phishing.

**Treatment owner:** Head of IT Operations

**Target status:** Planned

**Residual-risk review:** After authentication and account-monitoring controls are implemented.

### Risk 3: Unauthorized access to employee and financial information

**Treatment option:** Treat

**Treatment actions:**

- Apply role-based and least-privilege access.
- Use multi-factor authentication for sensitive systems.
- Review access to HR and financial records regularly.
- Remove access immediately when employees leave.
- Monitor access to sensitive records.
- Maintain confidentiality requirements for employees and contractors.

**Treatment owner:** Finance Manager and HR Manager, supported by IT Operations

**Target status:** Planned

**Residual-risk review:** After access reviews, offboarding, and monitoring controls are implemented.

### Risk 4: Learning platform unavailability

**Treatment option:** Treat

**Treatment actions:**

- Maintain regular backups.
- Monitor platform and critical third-party services.
- Test backup restoration.
- Define recovery procedures.
- Prepare business-continuity and disaster-recovery plans.
- Review cloud and service-provider availability commitments.
- Define alternative communication procedures for students.

**Treatment owner:** Head of IT Operations

**Target status:** Planned

**Residual-risk review:** After backup restoration and recovery procedures are tested.

## Residual Risk Review

After treatment actions are implemented, Ikigai will reassess each risk using the approved likelihood and impact scales. The remaining risk will be recorded as residual risk.

Each residual-risk decision should record:

- Residual likelihood
- Residual impact
- Residual risk rating
- Risk owner
- Approval authority
- Acceptance decision
- Review date
- Reason for acceptance, if applicable
